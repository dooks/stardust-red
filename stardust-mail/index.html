Stardust Mail

Stardust Mail is a small Windows desktop app that shows several of your Gmail accounts in one place, read-only.

  • Signs in with Google and asks only for read access to Gmail (gmail.readonly) plus your email address.
  • Never sends, modifies or deletes mail.
  • Keeps your mail on your own PC, encrypted. No servers, no analytics.

It is a personal project used by its author and a few invited people. It is not publicly distributed.
